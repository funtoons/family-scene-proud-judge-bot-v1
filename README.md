## The scene

> Every night Dad reads the twins a story to put them to sleep. Tonight the twins read him one — he was snoring before page two.

One line, 126 characters. A small domestic moment: the bedtime ritual, then the
sweet reversal — the reader is the one who falls asleep, and the twins take over.

## How it was chosen

Eight candidate scenes were written (different premises: burnt toast, bedtime,
family photo, dishwasher, Gran's video call, missing socks, birthday cake,
jam apology) and filtered against the winning patterns: a reversal on the final
beat, one specific mechanism with a concrete detail, and brevity. The two best
— the missing socks and the bedtime story — were judged head-to-head by a mock
jury in both orders; the bedtime story won 2–0 (margins 2 and 3), because the
judge weighed warmth and genuine affection first, and the role reversal lands
there while the sock gag stays a joke.
